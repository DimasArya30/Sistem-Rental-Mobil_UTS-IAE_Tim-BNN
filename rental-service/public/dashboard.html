Customer Service (Laravel)

Provider: GET/POST /api/customers

Consumer: GET /api/customers/{id}/rentals → RentalService

Car Service (CodeIgniter 4)

Provider: GET/POST/PUT /api/cars

Consumer: GET /api/cars/{id}/renters → CustomerService

Rental Service (Laravel)

Provider: GET /api/rentals

Consumer: POST /api/rentals → Customer + Car Service

Consumer: PUT /api/rentals/{id}/return → CarService
